# DEADWEIGHT: the full-playthrough quest log

Tick these off in order and you will have seen everything the build contains. Start the game with
`powershell -File tools/run.ps1`; add `-Level <id>` to jump straight into a level (`-Touch` for the
phone controls). Quests marked **(tell me)** come with a question: those answers are what the
tuning work is waiting on.

Controls: WASD move, mouse aim, hold and release left mouse to cast, right mouse locks or frees the
spool, hold E to reel, Q cuts the line, Space jumps, F swaps between the rod and the sidearm, R
reloads, Esc pauses.

## Act 0: Clock in

- [ ] **First boot.** Look at the title screen and its snowy valley behind the menu, then open
      Settings and set sensitivity, FOV and quality the way you like them.
- [ ] **Induction** (tutorial). Walk, cast, lock, reel, cut, cross a gap, swing from an anchor,
      ride an updraft, shoot the dummy and extract with the one case. Earns *Gone Fishing*,
      *Magnetic Personality* and *Inducted*. **(tell me)** Does the rod make sense by the end, or
      is there a step that teaches nothing?
- [ ] **Practice Range** (`-Level range`). There is no clock, so take your time. Climb the 29 m
      gantry, run the pillar slalom and smash the dummies with a swinging weight (*Dead Weight*).
      Try for 12 s in the air (*Hang Time*) and 45 m/s (*Terminal Velocity*).

## Act 1: The Department of Unfinished Gravity

Short raids, about one minute each. Grab every case, then hold the extraction pad while its clock
runs down.

- [ ] **Sluice Gate** (l01): 3 cases and 2 bonus caches. Earns *Bring Something Back*.
- [ ] **Pump Row** (l02): swing between the cyan anchor plates under the pipe racks.
- [ ] **Overflow** (l03): the deep water hurts. Use the islands, the vents and the towers.
      Clearing l01 to l03 earns *Drained*.
- [ ] **Slag Line** (l04): the Foundry, under the violet sky. Heat strips and your first drones.
- [ ] **Crane Yard** (l05): night. The anchors are on the crane arms.
- [ ] **Crucible** (l06): the teapot hall, the last sentries. Clearing l04 to l06 earns *Cooled
      Off*.
- [ ] **(tell me)** These are the levels you called "alpha level" on 4 November, and they are
      still the first six of the campaign. Is that the right first hour, or should the highlands
      come first?

## Act 2: Errands for Princess Capybara

Open valley, the floor is lava, and the hounds outrun you on foot. Runs take about three to six
minutes.

- [ ] **The Princess's Duck** (h01): bring the duck from the far forest and the firewood from the
      north woods. Once she has both, the meltwater rises. Bonus: her towel, on the giant duck's
      head in the west lake. **(tell me)** How long until the ice cracks, how thick the mines
      are, how fast the flood rises and how fast the hounds run.
- [ ] **The Bath Salts** (h02): three jars from the geyser basin. Stand on a vent while it
      hisses. Bonus: her sea sponge, on top of the 34 m basalt stack by the Kettle.
      **(tell me)** How hard the geysers throw, how often they go off, and how much the mud hurts.
- [ ] **The Yuzu Run** (h03): three yuzu on Marble Hill. Two are on ledges partway up the giant
      marble run, one under the tree inside the teacup rings. Bonus: the golden yuzu on the
      hopper roof. **(tell me)** How often marbles drop, how fast they roll, how tight the mine
      rings are.
- [ ] **The Ducklings** (h04): three ducklings along the crumbling aqueduct. Keep moving and rest
      on the stone. Bonus: the golden duckling on the soap bottle's pump. **(tell me)** How fast
      the collapse wave runs, how high the deck is, how big the slabs are.
- [ ] **Starfall** (h05, new): night falls over the highlands. Bring her three fallen stars:
      - the top of the Gravity Works: walk the spiral ramp, or ride the PUBLIC LIFT geyser and
        cast onto the deck;
      - Star Hill, where the marbles roll at you as you climb;
      - the smoking crater, inside two rings of teacups.

      Each star you carry makes the rest fall at you (a gold ring and a ray of light, then the
      strike), so stay in the air on the way back. Bonus: the North Star, on the crow's nest at
      the top of the mast. **(tell me)** Is the starfall exciting or just unfair? How often should
      the stars come, and how big should the blast be?
- [ ] **The ending.** Winning Starfall shows THE END and a CREDITS button. Read the credits.
      Earns *Bath Time*.
- [ ] **The Highlands preview** (`-Level valley`): the practice valley. No clock, one practice
      geyser, the duck.

## Act 3: Mastery

- [ ] **The Workshop.** Spend salvage on all six tracks: spool, reel, sinker, plating, quickdraw,
      sidearm. Max them all for *Fully Rigged*.
- [ ] **Bonus caches.** Every level from l01 to h05 has at least one. Most are rod-only.
- [ ] **Medals.** Gold on any level for *Gold Standard*. Records shows your times.
- [ ] **Style.** On a highland errand, push the air multiplier to x5 and kill something with a
      body slam at speed.
- [ ] **Untouched** (extract with enemies and take no damage) and **Ghost** (extract without
      firing the sidearm). Both count on the highland errands now.
- [ ] **Difficulty.** Play one level on each difficulty setting.

## Act 4: Other ways to play

- [ ] **Gamepad.** Play one raid and one errand on a controller.
- [ ] **Touch controls** (`-Touch`): play the tutorial and one errand with the on-screen pads.
      **(tell me)** Are the pads where your thumbs are?
- [ ] **Low quality.** Set quality to Low and play h01. Snowfall and the tub steam turn off. It
      should still look like the same game.
- [ ] **Phone (Q1).** Install the APK on your phone and note the frame rate on h01 and h05.

## What you cannot earn yet

*Master Angler* (gold everywhere), *Scavenger* (every bonus cache) and *Over the Top* (the
Spillway) all count the three Spillway levels l07 to l09. Those are planned but not built, so
nobody can earn these three yet. It is your call: build the Spillway, or change the three
achievements to count the levels that exist.
