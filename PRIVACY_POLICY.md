# DEADWEIGHT privacy policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026

This policy covers the game DEADWEIGHT on PC (Steam) and Android (Google Play). In short: the game
collects nothing about you and never connects to the internet.

## What the game collects

Nothing. DEADWEIGHT does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.
It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking and no accounts. The game does not use the
network at all; the Android version doesn't even request internet access. If you buy the game, or
anything inside it, Steam or Google Play handles the payment, and we never receive your payment
details.

## What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocks, upgrades, best times and medals) and your settings in a file
on your own device. It also keeps a local log file that helps with troubleshooting. Neither ever
leaves your device unless you choose to send it to us.

- **Android:** in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
- **Windows:** in `%APPDATA%\DEADWEIGHT\`. Uninstalling through Steam does not remove this folder;
  delete it yourself to remove your progress.
- **Linux and Steam Deck:** in `~/.local/share/DEADWEIGHT/`, which you can likewise delete.

## Stores and platforms

You download the game through Steam or Google Play. Those services may collect information about
your purchase and use of the game (for example installs, playtime or crash reports) under their own
privacy policies, which we don't control:

- Valve / Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you choose to send us a message or a log file, we use it only to answer you and fix problems,
and we delete it when we no longer need it.

## Children's privacy

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum
age that applies where you live).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Because the game collects
nothing, a change could only happen if a future update added a feature that needed it, and we would
describe it here first.
