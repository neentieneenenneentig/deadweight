## Description

DEADWEIGHT is a first-person movement game about a fishing rod that casts impossibly heavy
weights. Your legs are slow and the ground is hostile, so you travel by casting a weight, locking
the spool and letting it drag you into the sky. Reel in to climb, cut loose to keep your
momentum, recast in mid-air. Raid the Department of Unfinished Gravity, an absurdist public works
department under a painted afternoon sky, for salvage. Outfly porcelain tea hounds, clockwork
sentries and ceramic drones, and hold the extraction pad until the clock runs down.

## Setting

The Department of Unfinished Gravity keeps its offices in giant empty chairs, its doors open onto
nothing, and its notices are deadpan and gravely polite. The tone is quiet, solemn and faintly comic.

## Features

- Rope physics you can master: tension-only line, inherited momentum, casting recoil, free spool,
  lock, reel and cut.
- Short raids of one to four minutes with bronze, silver and gold medal times.
- Hand-built levels, a tutorial and a practice range.
- Weight smashes and a sidearm against porcelain and clockwork guards.
- Workshop upgrades for spool, reel, sinker, plating, quickdraw and sidearm.
- Mouse and keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; Steam Deck friendly.
- Fully offline, with no ads, accounts or data collection.

## History

DEADWEIGHT began as a one-level prototype built around a single question: what if the heaviest
thing you can throw is also how you fly? The models, animation and audio are generated by the
game's own code. Its sky, porcelain surface and vacant-chair mural are original AI-generated
textures, disclosed on the Steam page with their prompts kept in `assets/dreamscape/PROMPTS.md`.
