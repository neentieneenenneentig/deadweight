## Description

DEADWEIGHT is a first-person movement game about a fishing rod that casts impossibly heavy
weights. Your legs are slow and the floor is lava, so you travel by casting a weight, locking
the spool and letting it drag you into the sky. Reel in to climb, cut loose to keep your
momentum, recast in mid-air. Run absurd errands across the snowy highlands for Princess Capybara,
who is soaking in her hot tub and keeps losing things, over cracking ice, teacup mines, rolling
marbles and geysers. Below the snowline, raid the Department of Unfinished Gravity for salvage,
outfly porcelain tea hounds, clockwork sentries and ceramic drones, and hold the extraction pad
until the clock runs down.

## Setting

The highlands are a cold, painted alpine valley of snowfields, frozen lakes and dark pines, where a
capybara princess sends you after her rubber duck, her bath salts, her yuzu, her duck's
ducklings and, on the night the department finally finishes gravity, three fallen stars. Below them, the Department of Unfinished Gravity keeps building long after anyone
remembers why: sluice gates and pump rows in the frozen Floodworks, kilns and cranes in the Foundry
under a violet nightmare sky. Its doors open onto nothing, and its notices are deadpan and gravely
polite. The tone is deadpan and absurd, with a quiet, solemn streak.

## Features

- Rope physics you can master: tension-only line, inherited momentum, casting recoil, free spool,
  lock, reel and cut.
- Five open highland errands, six department raids, a tutorial and a practice range.
- Runs of one to six minutes with bronze, silver and gold medal times.
- The floor is lava: cracking ice and paving, teacup mines, rolling marbles, geysers and floods.
- A style multiplier that climbs the longer you stay airborne.
- Weight smashes and a sidearm against porcelain and clockwork guards.
- Workshop upgrades for spool, reel, sinker, plating, quickdraw and sidearm.
- Mouse and keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; Steam Deck friendly.
- Fully offline, with no ads, accounts or data collection.

## History

DEADWEIGHT began as a one-level prototype built around a single question: what if the heaviest
thing you can throw is also how you fly? The models, animation and audio are generated by the
game's own code. Six textures (three sky panoramas, the slate and porcelain surfaces and the
vacant-chair mural) are original AI-generated images, disclosed on the Steam page with their
prompts kept in `assets/dreamscape/PROMPTS.md`.
