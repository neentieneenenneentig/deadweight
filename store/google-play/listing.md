Paste into Play Console: Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing. Character counts were
measured with tools; Play's limits are in brackets.

**First, the owner must choose the Android business model (PLAN §4, step O3b). It can't be
undone:** Play never lets a free app become paid, and testers in a closed test must buy a paid
app (only internal testers get it free).

- **Model A, free to try (recommended):** a free app; the tutorial, the first levels and the
  practice range are free, and one in-app purchase unlocks the full game. Closed testers play for
  free, and license testers can test the purchase without being charged. Needs task P1 (Play
  Billing unlock) before launch.
- **Model B, paid up front:** simpler, but all 12+ closed-test testers have to buy the game (you can
  refund them afterwards in the Order management page).
